      Questions people ask first

      Anything else? Write to help@paywallet.app and a person answers within a day.

      Does it cost anything?

      The app is free, and sending money to friends is free. Card payments abroad carry a 1% exchange fee, shown before you pay.

      How fast does money arrive?

      Between PayWallet users, instantly. To a bank account, usually within the hour on working days.

      What if I lose my phone?

      Sign in on the web or call us to freeze your card and sign out every device. Your money stays where it is.

      Is my money protected?

      Balances are held with partner banks in safeguarded accounts, separate from PayWallet's own money.
